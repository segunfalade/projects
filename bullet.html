DAY 1

The Missing Tooth

A great bullet doesn't ask for permission. It deletes the one piece your mind needs, and your cognition starts working before you choose to care.

The Formula

  • Concrete Promise — a tangible, recognizable outcome.
  • Hidden Key — the mechanism, word, or condition that unlocks it.
  • The Withholding — you name the promise and hint at the key, then delete the key.

EXAMPLE

"Say the secret word and rental car costs less."

Missing Tooth: What is the word?

The Psychology

This is the Zeigarnik Effect. Unfinished mental loops demand closure.

Your mind builds answers before you consent. That non-consensual engagement is your leverage.

"A great bullet leaves out the one piece you want most, and that little gap starts to ache, like a missing tooth."

The North Star Examples

The Golden Rule

Be hyper-specific about the outcome. Be hyper-mysterious about the path. Never explain both. Always delete the path.

DO

Name the concrete result. Use real nouns: bills, food, word, phrase.

DO

Withhold exactly one critical mechanism, list, or condition.

DON'T

Be vague ("secrets to success"). Don't include the answer in the same sentence.
