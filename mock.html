Relationship OS Categorize, tier, and schedule pings
Never Eat Alone-inspired routine: sort every case into a relationship bucket, assign a tier, then use the ping rhythm to give value before you need anything. Tier 1 gets frequent thoughtful touches, Tier 2 gets consistent nurture, Tier 3 stays warm without becoming admin-heavy.

Relationship command center

Ping routine

Tier 1 · Inner circlePing weekly to every 2 weeks

High-value relationships, active opportunities, mentors, partners, and people you want to be meaningfully close to. Every touch should be personal or useful.

Tier 2 · Active nurturePing monthly to every 6 weeks

Warm prospects, collaborators, and connectors. Keep context alive with insight, congratulations, thoughtful comments, or relevant introductions.

Tier 3 · Light touchPing quarterly to twice a year

Weak ties, dormant contacts, and future opportunities. Stay visible without forcing urgency. Use lightweight value and memory hooks.
