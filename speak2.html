The three rules

SOCIAL REPS ARE MEETINGS

Put them in your calendar

Like a client call. They don't count if they're ad-hoc and unlogged.

RECORD EVERYTHING

Voice memos + camera

If it isn't recorded, it didn't happen — for evaluation purposes. Log the count.

THE SUNDAY REVIEW IS THE PROGRAM

Never skip it

You can miss a drill; you don't miss the review. That's what makes it training.

Scripts (adapt, don't recite)

60-second pitch
Who you help → what you do → one proof point → what makes it different → soft close. Close: "I'd love to hear more about what you're working on."
Discovery call (10 min)
Thank + agenda → listen (HEAR) → 2 clarifying questions → 90-sec approach → confirm next step + date → close warm.
Referral email
"Hi [Name] — you've been one of my favorite projects. If anyone in your network is struggling with [problem], would you be open to introducing me? No pressure either way. Thanks again for [specific thing] on [project]. — [You]"
48-hour follow-up
"Great catching up on [day]. Really enjoyed [specific thing]. As promised, here's [resource/intro/thing]. Let's keep in touch — I'll check back in a few weeks."
5 small-talk openers
"How did you end up at [event]?" / "Is this your first time here?" / Comment true + question / "I'm [name] — [line]. What do you do?" / Queue: observation + "Are you a regular?"
3 exit lines
"I'm going to say hello to [person] — great talking, [name]." / "I should get going, but I really enjoyed this. Let's keep in touch — [handle]." / "I have a hard stop — thank you for [specific thing]."

FORD bank (fill 12 questions)

F: Where from? Grow up around? Take after who? O: Most interesting now? What got you in? Typical day? R: Weekend routine? Hobbies / reading / travel? D: Project most excited? Two-year vision? Dream if unlimited budget/time? Bridge: After any answer, ask: "What's the most rewarding part of that?"

Listener ask (recruit by Day 20)

"I'm doing 60 days to get better at talking. I need 2 × 30 min in October — I'll talk, you'll answer 5 questions. Can you do that?"

Feedback form (Milestone #1 & #2)

Give each trusted listener this 5-question form (~3 min): 1) Main point in your words? 2) What held attention? 3) Where did mind wander? 4) Pace /5, Clarity /5? 5) One improvement for next talk?

Social battery management

  • Cap: max 3 in-person reps/week; no two same day (unless full event).
  • After in-person rep: 20 min recharge.
  • Written note = half a rep — top up low-battery weeks.
  • If anxiety >7/10, shrink to half length instead of skipping.
