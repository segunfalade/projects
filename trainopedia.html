★ The Objection Killers

The Questions Smart Parents Ask

Is this a replacement for school?
No — and we’re deliberate about that. School handles certification; we handle capability. Trainopedia is engineered to sit alongside school, not on top of it.
How much time does it take?
3–5 hours per week, fully flexible and built around the school calendar — including exam periods.
Isn’t this just more screen time?
It’s the opposite. Most screen time is consumption — scrolling and watching. Our missions are creation: writing, building, analyzing, presenting. Your child will spend fewer hours on a screen, and every hour will produce something.
My child already gets excellent grades. Why do they need this?
Grades are the local ceiling. A straight-A student with no portfolio is, to a global evaluator, identical to a thousand other straight-A students. We turn “brilliant on paper” into “proven in practice.”
My child isn’t “technical.” Can they still succeed?
Every track starts from zero — no coding required to begin. The assessment places them where their natural strengths already lie, whether that’s logic, communication, or analysis.
How do I know it’s working?
You’ll receive a Monthly Talent Audit showing skill growth against global benchmarks — plus a portfolio you can open any day and see with your own eyes.
What does it cost?
Less than typical extra-lesson fees — for skills no lesson teacher can offer. ₦85,000 / term (12 weeks) ₦240,000 / academic year Sibling discount available

Every term includes the strengths assessment, all Practical Missions, the Digital Career Portfolio, and twelve Monthly Talent Audits a year.
